# Spend model-selection effort on prespecified comparisons, not rank noise

Use rank sensitivity to identify a shortlist, then collect paired task evidence for the actual selection. A descriptive reversal is a prompt to investigate, not proof of superiority.

**Observed evidence:** None of nine selected reversals survives Holm correction over all 1,953 searched pairs. Missing item-paired covariance prevents exact paired inference from the aggregate table.

**Decision owner:** Model procurement lead (proposed role, not a client).

**Base planning case:** Aggregate screen then paired shortlist. This is conditional on the assumptions, not deployment approval.

| Alternative | Economic cost | Cash outlay | Reviewer hours | Passes risk and capacity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Full paired evaluation before each selection | $29,700 | $7,200 | 180.0 | True |
| Aggregate screen then paired shortlist | $26,040 | $11,880 | 96.0 | True |
| Select the headline leaderboard winner | $19,320 | $0 | 12.0 | False |

Economic cost includes valued staff capacity and scenario incident losses. Cash outlay excludes salaried capacity and contingent losses. These are not realized savings.

**Sensitivity across 27 equally enumerated scenarios:** Full paired evaluation before each selection: 2, NO FEASIBLE OPTION: 18, Aggregate screen then paired shortlist: 7. Grid counts are not probabilities.

**Synthetic product experiment:** HOLD. Primary effect 3.7%; 95% interval [-0.2%, 7.5%]. Guardrail effect 1.0%; interval [0.4%, 1.8%]. These numbers test the decision logic, not product performance.

**Priority order:**

1. Choose the relevant task and candidate pair before confirmatory evaluation.
2. Separate exploratory rank sensitivity from the selection test.
3. Do not promote a model merely because a different aggregate benchmark changes its rank.

**What would reverse the recommendation:** At low decision volume, fixed shortlist tooling can cost more than direct paired evaluation; missing paired evidence prevents a definitive winner.

**Remaining uncertainty:** Assumed procurement-loss rates are independent scenario inputs, not measured by leaderboard inversions.
